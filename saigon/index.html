The material

Colour belongs to the light, never to the interface.

One backdrop carries the entire chromatic budget of the page — greens dissolving into amber, anchored in a molten oxblood that sits at the bottom of the range rather than the middle of it. Everything else on this page is black, white, or one of five greys.

The body is rendered per-pixel and reacts to the pointer: the material is displaced around where you are and keeps running everywhere else, so moving through it disturbs the flow rather than dragging the picture.

What the system forbids

Sharp everywhere, except where it is fully round.

Radius jumps from 0 to 75 with nothing in between. No shadow and no elevation anywhere — separation is a one-pixel hairline. No gradient on a control. No chromatic interface colour at any size.

A visual and motion study built to the monopo saigon style reference — its palette, type scale, spacing rhythm and motion curve.

Roobert substituted with Inter. Raleway appears once, as a heading accent. system-ui carries every label at 9 and 16px.

Motion runs on cubic-bezier(0.19, 1, 0.22, 1) at 0.8 to 1.25 seconds. Nothing here snaps.

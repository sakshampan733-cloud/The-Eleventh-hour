Jarvis

A private personal assistant that runs entirely on its owner's own Mac. It is a personal project, not a public service.

What it does

  • Answers by voice, keeps a to-do list, notes and reminders.
  • Reads its owner's Google Classroom coursework and announcements, and their Gmail, so it can say what's due and what's new.
  • Works with its owner's own apps, TV and files on the same Mac.

Privacy

Jarvis has no servers. Everything it reads stays on the owner's Mac, and Google access is read-only. See the privacy policy.

Privacy policy

Jarvis · last updated 30 October 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own Mac. It is used only by its owner, for their own accounts. This policy explains what it does with Google data.

What Jarvis accesses

  • Gmail, read-only: recent messages, to tell the owner what's new and important.
  • Google Classroom, read-only: courses, coursework, announcements and teachers' names, to track what's due.
  • The account's email address, to tell connected accounts apart.

Jarvis cannot send email, change anything in Gmail or Classroom, or submit work.

Where the data goes

  • Nowhere. Data is fetched directly from Google to the owner's Mac and kept there, in a private folder only the owner's user account can read.
  • Jarvis has no servers, no analytics and no advertising. Google data is never sold, shared, or sent to any third party.
  • Its AI runs on the same Mac; Google data is not sent to any outside AI service or used to train models.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's Mac.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Deleting Jarvis's folder on the Mac removes everything it stored.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
